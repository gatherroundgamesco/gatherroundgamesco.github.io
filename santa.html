Reveal links and privacy: FAQ

Are the reveal links encrypted?

No. Reveal links are scrambled, not encrypted. The pairing is written into the link itself in a scrambled form so a quick glance won't spoil it. Anyone with a little technical know-how who has the link can unscramble it and see who the person is buying for. There is no password and no secret key.

So can the organizer see who has whom?

Yes, if they want to. The organizer has every link and could unscramble any of them. The tool keeps the pairs off the organizer's screen so nobody spoils it by accident, but it can't stop someone determined to peek. If even the organizer must not know, use the printable slips and have someone else hand them out, or let each person draw their own folded slip.

Is anything sent to a server or stored?

No. The draw happens in your browser. Names, wish lists, and pairs are not uploaded, saved, or emailed by us, and we use no cookies or tracking. The pairing lives in the part of the link after the #, which browsers don't send to the website when the page loads.

Who else could see a link?

Anyone you share it with, or anyone who can see the message you send it in (a group chat, a shared inbox, a phone's lock-screen preview). Send each link only to its own person, one-to-one. Link previews in some chat apps may open the page, but the pairing is not part of what the server receives.

What if I redraw?

A new draw makes new links. Old links still show the old pairing, so tell everyone to use only the newest link.

Should I put sensitive information in wish lists?

No. Wish lists travel inside the links with the same scrambled-not-encrypted protection. Keep them to gift ideas: no addresses, phone numbers, or anything private.
